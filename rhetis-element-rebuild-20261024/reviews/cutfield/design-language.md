# Design language

Lower-right28px chamfers define fields and phase cards.54px figures are light, with18px/650 names and16px body at1.45 leading, while the very large page titles are light at350 weight. Fine1–1.5px strokes make rising diagonal arrows and straight timelines. The page owns its deep accent scene; faces are independently page/neutral/primary/secondary roles. The geometry is flat, sharp and open, without shadows or generic rounded enclosures.

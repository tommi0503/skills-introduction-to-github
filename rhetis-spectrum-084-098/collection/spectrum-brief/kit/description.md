Spectrum Brief distils seven corporate, research and healthcare reference decks into a precise reporting language. White rectangular fields, a fine mint rail, dense navy headings and generous clear space give information a stable frame. Blue and cyan support quantitative content; green signals progress, while a restrained lavender tone preserves the healthcare references. Corners are almost square, diagrams use fine straight connectors, figures are substantial, and emphasis comes from flat colour rather than ornament. Dark mode uses layered navy surfaces and brighter mint with separately tuned ink colours. The sharply geometric corporate layouts form the common structure; photographic, 3D and particle material is represented by neutral seats.

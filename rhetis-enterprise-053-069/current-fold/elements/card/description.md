The card element translates the Current Fold language into variants `.on` (emphasis: accent fill) `.quiet` `.line` (outlined) `.tone-1…5`. Text parts inside `.parts`: `.kick` (small line above) `.fig` (the figure; `<small>` for its unit) `.name` `.note` `.mark` (icon or number).. Fine open rules, pale planes, disciplined left alignment and compact utility shapes carry the hierarchy; a single clear accent marks the primary item. Narrow containers step typography down through the kit scale.

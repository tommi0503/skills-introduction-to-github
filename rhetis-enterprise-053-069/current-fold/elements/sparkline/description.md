The sparkline element translates the Current Fold language into a mini trend with no axes or labels, set inline beside a figure: nests inside a `.stat` or `.card` `.parts` (after `.fig`) or stands alone in a small cell; `.spark.bars` mini bars; `.spark.up` / `.spark.down` colours the trend as good or bad.. Fine open rules, pale planes, disciplined left alignment and compact utility shapes carry the hierarchy; a single clear accent marks the primary item. Narrow containers step typography down through the kit scale.

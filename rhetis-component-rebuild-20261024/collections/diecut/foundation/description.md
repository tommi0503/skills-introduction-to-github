Measured full-bleed cover furniture, compact chapter navigation, a rotated contents rail and an independent twelve-column by six-row body grid. Normal pages use 72px horizontal and 44px vertical insets; furniture never changes element CSS.

`basic-notch.html` demonstrates `.deco` with palette-filled SVG primitives: the rounded mechanical plate, curved side notches and four circular holes. Flat or paired page faces are also explicit primitive background furniture, without page-number selectors. `cover-flat.html` demonstrates the full-bleed flat cover, right partial `seat.back`, two-phrase kicker, thin wordmark, introductory label, two-line `tag.hl` and metadata; covers have no head/body.

The wordmark and rail use the registered Pretendard Variable font extension. The 128px type ceiling remains in force; exact 142px and 146px source typography is documented as a contract gap, with no transforms used to fake larger text.

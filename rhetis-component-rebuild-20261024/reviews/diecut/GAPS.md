## Exact type-cap gaps (foundation revision 016)

- **01-04, cover wordmark**: source `MIRIROBOTICS` is Pretendard Variable 400 at 142px. The shipped `.title` uses 128px, the largest contract step. No transform or horizontal scaling is used. Proposed contract extension: `<h1 class="wordmark" data-type-size="142">MIRIROBOTICS</h1>`.
- **02-03, rotated contents rail**: source `CONTENTS` is Pretendard Variable 400 at 146px. The existing `.rail` preserves rotation, position, tracking and wording at the 128px ceiling. Proposed extension: `<aside class="rail-wordmark" data-type-size="146">CONTENTS</aside>`.

The partial cover media, two highlight bands, notch plates and holes, flat gray/light faces, paired ellipses, closing split panels and contents corner triangle are now represented by declared foundation furniture. They are not gaps. The `.deco` SVG contains only measured primitive geometry with palette-role fills; it never contains an embedded source bitmap. See each background/furniture record in map.json. Ordinary header-flow differences remain review notes rather than invented contract gaps.

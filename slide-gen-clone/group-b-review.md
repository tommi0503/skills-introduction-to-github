# r12–r22 visual review

All originals were visually opened and inspected before implementation. All slide canvases use shared 1280 × 720 primitives, with no rotation. Unsupported photographs, screenshots, faceted illustrations, gradient backgrounds and graphic marks use light gray image regions.

Two render/comparison inspection passes were performed for every deck. First pass identified typography sizing, r14 line-wrap overlap, r19 quote clipping and r20 oversized/cropped closing title. Second pass confirmed fixed r14 wrapping, complete r19 quote and complete r20 labels. The final second-pass correction adjusts r13 numeric baseline, long paragraph bounds, the collage founder quote logo, and narrow chart labels; parent aggregate validation supplies the third and final render.

Only recognizable visible headings and structure are included; missing panel contents cannot be recovered. Normalizing those partial crops to 16:9 makes direct comparison contact sheets appear stretched on the reference side. r14 intentionally clips an oversize title at its right edge exactly as the split source panel does. Fine print in low-resolution collages remains approximate, rather than a claim of exact transcription. Unsupported decorative pixel type is represented using installed sans typography.

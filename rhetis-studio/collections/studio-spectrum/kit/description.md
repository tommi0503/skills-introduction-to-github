Studio Spectrum is an editorial presentation language distilled from the ten supplied business and portfolio references. Quiet white space, flat surfaces, fine horizontal rules and crisp near-square corners give information a composed rhythm. Navy carries the default emphasis; muted red, fresh lime and soft green offer distinct narrative tones. Large confident figures sit beside compact explanatory text. Color is applied in deliberate areas rather than decoration. Dark mode uses a deep blue paper, lifted slate surfaces and brighter restrained accents, with an independently tuned contrast hierarchy.

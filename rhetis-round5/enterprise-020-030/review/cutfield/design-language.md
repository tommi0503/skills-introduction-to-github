# Design language — round5

Lower-right28px chamfers define fields and phase cards.54px figures are light, with18px/650 names and16px body at1.45 leading, while the very large page titles are light at350 weight. Fine1–1.5px strokes make rising diagonal arrows and straight timelines. The page owns its deep accent scene; faces are independently page/neutral/primary/secondary roles. The geometry is flat, sharp and open, without shadows or generic rounded enclosures. The individual source-backed elements are measured independently; absent contracts are inferred only after those elements, without reusing a previous kit stylesheet.


Source measurements, independent designs, precision typography and responsive panel geometry are listed in map.json and ORIGINS.md. Only source-backed designs and their necessary dependencies are retained. Type tokens cover8–256.

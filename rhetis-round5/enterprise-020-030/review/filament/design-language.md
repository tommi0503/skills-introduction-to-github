# Design language — round5

Light editorial type,1px rounded outlines and12px corners contrast with large uninterrupted page space. Italic13px credit lines and fine curved ornaments are secondary devices. Body paragraphs occupy narrow measured columns, and the service rows keep their original line rhythm. The page, deep accent text and accent fill/on pair are separate roles. Oversize display titles and curved text seals that the contract cannot carry are recorded as gaps. The individual source-backed elements are measured independently; absent contracts are inferred only after those elements, without reusing a previous kit stylesheet.


Source measurements, independent designs, precision typography and responsive panel geometry are listed in map.json and ORIGINS.md. Only source-backed designs and their necessary dependencies are retained. Type tokens cover8–256.

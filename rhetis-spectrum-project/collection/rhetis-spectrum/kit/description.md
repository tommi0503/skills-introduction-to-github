Spectrum combines a clean corporate grid with confident blue accents, softly tinted information planes, fine dividing rules and generous white space. Mint and coral secondary tones retain the vocabulary of the marketing and year-end reference decks. Rounded capsules distinguish actions and concise labels; larger information blocks use restrained corners. Typography is compact, calm and strongly hierarchical, with bold figures and readable Korean business copy. Dark mode uses layered slate surfaces and light blue emphasis rather than color inversion.

# Group B — l25–l36

Scope: 12 brochure templates, 24 unfolded source sides, 72 independently clipped fold pages. Own files are patch-b.ts and this record. All 24 source sides were opened individually before implementation. Each completed round included opening all 24 individual unfolded comparison images and all 12 six-fold contact grids. Review-1 and review-2 completed; no third agent render was made. The parent final pass must validate the late corrections listed below. TypeScript validation passes.

Native source coordinates preserve the three panel widths and full height. Photos, QR codes, custom image icons, illustration, and complex patterned backgrounds use solid #e5e5e5 placeholders. Readable labels, menu prices, schedules, and chart/table entries are text and primitives. Fragmentary OCR was removed and manual transcription substituted; some very small body copy is natural wording as authorized, rather than asserted verbatim transcription.

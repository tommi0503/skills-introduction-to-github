FAQ

What is Monologue?+

Monologue is a voice productivity app for builders. It combines dictation in any app, voice notes, and bot-free meeting notes in one product. It turns speech into clear text, transcribes and summarizes longer recordings, and lets you use saved Notes with AI agents through MCP, API, and CLI.

Which devices does Monologue support?+

Monologue has native apps for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Saved Notes, custom modes, and your dictionary sync across your Apple devices, so you can dictate at your desk, capture a thought on your watch, and return to the transcript later on another device.

Can Monologue record both sides of a meeting without a bot?+

Yes. On Mac, Monologue can record your microphone and system audio together, capturing your voice and the other people in a meeting without adding a bot to the call. You must grant the optional Screen Recording permission before Monologue can capture system audio.

Does Monologue work offline, and how private is it?+

Monologue offers local transcription models on Mac that can turn speech into text without an internet connection. Smart formatting and syncing still require a connection. For Dictation, Monologue does not save audio or transcripts on its servers, and its AI providers process dictation with zero data retention. Saved Notes are stored separately so they can sync across your devices.

Can I use my Monologue notes with AI agents?+

Yes. Monologue can make saved Notes available to supported AI agents and tools through MCP, API, and CLI access. An agent can search or retrieve transcripts and summaries you have already captured, so you can turn conversations into meeting minutes, action items, or other work without copying and pasting the original transcript.

Business Prism turns the reference set's business reports into a crisp, reusable presentation language. White fields, precise rectangular panels, quiet gray rules, dark blue title bands, clear cyan data accents, and occasional warm orange milestones establish an orderly rhythm. Headings are compact and confident, numerical figures are open and prominent, and fine borders separate evidence without adding visual noise. Corners are nearly square; circles are reserved for people, shares, and relationship diagrams. The dark mode uses navy fields and pale blue emphasis with independent, legible contrast.

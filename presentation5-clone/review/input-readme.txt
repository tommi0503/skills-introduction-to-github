ENVATO PRESENTATION IMAGE REFERENCES | 2026-10-06

Reviewed: 205 unique deck detail pages using the cloud browser.
Shortlist: 64 decks; 238 public detail-preview image requests.
Retrieved: 215 images; 23 requests blocked by the browser and omitted.
Final selection: 47 decks / 117 unique images.

Selection: distinctive editorial/brand layouts and/or varied charts, tables,
processes, timelines, comparative diagrams and dashboards. Final files show
front-facing slides/contact sheets. Tilted slide mockups, device-only display
mockups, overlapping marketing covers and incomplete/cropped sheets were omitted.

Images are the original bytes downloaded from the largest srcset entry actually
offered on the opened detail page (1370w request; native files may be smaller).
No thumbnail URLs, upscaling, straightening, cropping or recompression were used.
The actual pixel dimensions, source URL and SHA-256 are recorded in the manifest.
These are public preview images, not exported PPTX slide masters. Some original
previews contain shadows or watermarks. PowerPoint-filtered results also included
titles mentioning Google Slides or Keynote; titles are retained verbatim.

visual_review.pdf: one full-resolution image per A3 landscape page; zoom to inspect.
image_manifest.csv/json: per-image source, dimensions, hashes and deck identifiers.
reviewed_205_decks.csv: complete reviewed-detail-page ledger.
unavailable_previews.json: the 23 omitted browser-blocked requests.

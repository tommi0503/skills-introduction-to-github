A card that is mostly open page: a pale square of decoration sits at its top-left corner and the text starts offset over it, a small light secondary kicker, a figure in the condensed display face, a bold name in the accent and a light secondary note, all ranged left. With a mark the card centres on a solid accent disc holding the pictogram or numeral in the background colour. Emphasis floods the card with the accent and turns the text to the background colour; quiet cards are a flat pale tile; outlined cards draw a hairline box offset from the decorative square; toned cards colour the disc and add a short thick bar under the name.

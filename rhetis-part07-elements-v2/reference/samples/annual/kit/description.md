# Annual

A calm, airy business-report look on a warm cream page with one deep accent and a mid grey. Body pages open on a very large outlined section numeral, hairline only, with the page title in a condensed squared display face set beside it on its foot; elsewhere a pale warm square sits offset behind the start of a heading or a text block. Charts are rings and discs: thick accent rings around white faces, a soft wide ring circled by a dotted orbit with small dot nodes, rounded segment bars stacked like tablets and rows of person pictograms. Body text is small and light, tightly tracked, in the accent or in grey; names are bold. Pictures are flat square-cornered tiles, circles for portraits. The cover is split: a cream top with the condensed title, a solid accent band below carrying the light lead. Dark mode is the band's world: the page takes the accent, the cream becomes the ink. Do Hyeon for display, Noto Sans KR for text.

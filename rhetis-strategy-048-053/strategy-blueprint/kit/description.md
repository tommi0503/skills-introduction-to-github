Strategy Blueprint is a precise editorial business system derived from six Korean strategy and company-profile decks. White space, slate rules, navy and sky-blue emphasis, soft rectangular panels and circular index markers create a calm, analytical rhythm. An earth-orange tone recalls the monochrome progress report. Large condensed-feeling numerals sit beside clear Korean text. Dark mode uses ink-blue surfaces and luminous blue accents, with independent contrast pairs rather than a mechanical inversion.

## Required visual fidelity gate

1. Review every page at native 1280×720, showing original, replica and reassembled page together. Do not rely on contact sheets or overall deck appearance.
2. Compare original to replica first, then replica to reassembly. This separates reference interpretation errors from losses introduced by library abstraction.
3. Use a 50% overlay and a side-by-side view. Mask only the user-authorized gray placeholders for media, complex illustrations and raster icons. Do not mask typography, simple shapes or connections.
4. Record principal block bounds, margins and whitespace; text size, weight, baselines and line breaks; diagram heights, arrow endpoints and directions; and the count and position of icon/content rows.
5. Treat a missing structural row, incorrect connection or materially altered hierarchy as a failure even when CSS and overflow audits pass. Resolve the cause at the relevant stage before judging color or decoration.

Rhetis Report Suite translates the source decks into a precise editorial reporting system. Fine dark outlines, restrained corners, generous white space, modular tables and strong numerical typography create a calm hierarchy. Cobalt carries the primary emphasis; pale yellow marks conclusions, with sage, warm stone and lime extending the language to diagrams and comparison. Dark surfaces use a deliberately lighter ink and brighter accent palette, preserving the crisp report character without photographic decoration. The collection is designed for Korean business reporting, with compact labels and readable, comfortably spaced body copy.
